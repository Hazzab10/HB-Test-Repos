## Question 1
**What is the capital city of Australia?**

A) Sydney
B) Melbourne
C) Canberra
D) Brisbane

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

**Answer: C) Canberra**

While Sydney is the largest city and Melbourne is the second-largest, Canberra is the capital of Australia.
</details>

## Question 2
**Which river is the longest in the world?**

A) Amazon River
B) Nile River
C) Yangtze River
D) Mississippi River

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

**Answer: B) Nile River**

The Nile River in Africa is approximately 6,650 km (4,130 miles) long, making it the longest river in the world.
</details>

## Question 3
**How many countries does the Equator pass through?**

A) 7 countries
B) 10 countries
C) 13 countries
D) 16 countries

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

**Answer: C) 13 countries**

The Equator passes through 13 countries: Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil, São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Kenya, Somalia, Maldives, Indonesia, and Kiribati.
</details>
